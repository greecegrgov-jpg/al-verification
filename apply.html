IMPORTANT INFORMATION BEFORE APPLICATION

  1. Payment for the visa is not refunded in case of rejection of the application.
  2. Obtaining a visa is not a guarantee that the foreigner is allowed to enter the Republic of Albania.
  3. Visa applications must be completed online and are submitted at the applicant's own risk.
  4. During the visa application verification process, it is strictly forbidden for the applicant, the invitee, or any other subject to communicate with the responsible institutions, except when contacted by the relevant Diplomatic or Consular Mission.
  5. The evaluation process after payment is normally completed within the applicable processing period.
  6. In case of non-payment of the visa fee after the required period, the application will change to the appropriate status and may be automatically rejected.
  7. Applicants with dual citizenship must select the nationality of the passport they will use for travel.
  8. All documents uploaded must be translated where required and must comply with the specified document format.

PLEASE FOLLOW THE GUIDELINES BELOW WHEN UPLOADING THE REQUESTED DOCUMENTS IN YOUR VISA APPLICATION

  • All documents must be clear and readable.
  • Upload complete documents without missing pages.
  • Make sure the information on each document is clearly visible.
  • Documents must be uploaded in the required format.
  • Do not upload blurred, damaged or incomplete documents.
